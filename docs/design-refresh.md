# Bright category refresh — 23 September 2026

Visual references requested by the owner: [Jamba](https://www.jamba.com/menu), [Boost Juice](https://www.boostjuice.com.au/), and [Joe & the Juice](https://www.joejuice.com/). These are inspiration references, not copied site templates or brand assets.

The new palette combines citrus yellow, berry pink, apricot and mint. Category navigation, product cards and their button hover states share the same color associations. Dark ink text retains readable contrast on light surfaces.

Existing licensed/illustrative project photography is reused rather than generating new assets: category-specific photo panels and stronger fixed background layers change on category selection or swipe. All treats and text searches use the general mixed-menu artwork. Photographic edges are softly masked; pointer motion still uses the existing throttled transform-only handler and reduced-motion/touch safeguards.

The WhatsApp summary now separates customer/fulfillment details, numbered products, sizes, quantities, unit prices, line totals, total quantity, subtotal, and special requests. Delivery charges are not included until confirmed. Tests intercept checkout and never send real orders.
